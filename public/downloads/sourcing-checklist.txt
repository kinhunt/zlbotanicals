Sourcing checklist — botanical ingredients / 植物原料采购清单
Public buyer planning aid — not a COA, technical specification, certificate or company capability statement.
公开采购规划工具，并非分析证书、技术规格、认证证书或公司能力证明。

1. Identity / 原料身份
Product and botanical name, plant part, origin requirements, extraction solvent, carrier/excipients.
产品及植物学名称、使用部位、原产地要求、提取溶剂、载体或辅料。

2. Specification / 规格
Target marker/assay and test method, extract ratio, appearance, particle size, solubility or dispersibility.
目标标志物及含量、检测方法、提取比例、外观、粒径、溶解或分散性能。

3. Application and market / 应用及市场
Finished product, use level, process temperature/pH, packaging, target sales countries, ingredient and claims review.
终端产品、添加量、加工温度及pH、包装、目标销售国家、原料准入与宣称审查。

4. Quality evidence / 质量资料
Request the relevant grade TDS and batch-specific COA with methods and limits. Identify contaminants, microbiology, residual solvents and allergen information needed for your application.
索取对应规格的TDS及批次COA，核对方法与限值。列明用途所需的污染物、微生物、溶剂残留和过敏原资料。
If certification is required, verify legal entity, site, product scope, issuer and validity; do not infer coverage from a logo.
如需认证，请核实法人主体、场地、产品范围、发证机构及有效期；不可仅凭标识判断覆盖范围。
Documents and certifications are subject to availability and scope verification.
文件及认证以实际可提供情况及范围核查为准。

5. Evaluation / 评估
Agree sample availability, quantity, cost and freight in writing. Set acceptance criteria before bench and pilot trials; check stability in the final formulation and packaging.
书面确认样品可用性、数量、费用及运费。实验室及中试前设定验收标准，并在最终配方和包装中验证稳定性。

6. Commercial and delivery / 商务及交付
First order quantity and unit, annual estimate, destination, desired date, packaging, MOQ, price currency, Incoterms, payment terms and document requirements — confirm in writing before purchase.
首单数量及单位、预计年用量、目的地、期望日期、包装、起订量、报价币种、贸易术语、付款条件及文件要求——采购前书面确认。
