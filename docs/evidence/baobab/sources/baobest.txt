WHERE DOES BAOBAB POWDER COME FROM? Baobab powder is derived from the fruit of the baobab tree, which is native to Africa, particularly in countries like Senegal, South Africa, Zimbabwe and Malawi. The tree produces large, hard-shelled fruits that contain a naturally dehydrated pulp. This pulp is harvested, dried, and ground into a fine powder rich in nutrients, including vitamin C, calcium, and fiber. Our baobab powder is sourced from wild-harvested fruits, ensuring the natural environment remains undisturbed and promoting sustainability and quality.
