## Finite must-fix list — all six resolved in corrected copies

1. **Make the measured fraction explicit.** Table1 Mw is the weight-average molecular weight of recovered serum-pectin material, after dilution, separation and ethanol precipitation—not whole-paste molecular weight or dry-powder concentration. Content is mg GalA/g serum, not mg/g powder. DM is not methoxyl mass% or powder purity. The original sentence claiming the Results explains the percentage “basis” was too strong: Results supplies a percent sign and a methyl-esterification description, not a fully reproduced analytical denominator/protocol. Corrected copies describe only that level of evidence.[2]
2. **Separate break temperature from the complete process.** All four juices later undergo 65°C/120min vacuum concentration. Do not call the ultrasound routes low-temperature processing end-to-end. Explicitly distinguish wet concentrated paste rheology from the freeze-dried samples used for compositional analysis. No commercial spray-dried/reconstituted comparison, performance ratio or replacement factor follows.[2]
3. **Bring shear into the purchasing decision.** Original Methods are 25°C, 300s equilibration, 50s⁻¹/120s averaging; shear sweep is 0.01–500s⁻¹. Keep shear rate, geometry, timing and sample preparation beside temperature. No rpm→s⁻¹ or flow-distance→viscosity shortcut. °Brix is not a complete all-solids denominator. Added as editorial qualification guidance, not an experiment we performed.[2]
4. **Remove the currant-tomato directional section rather than pass an uninspected graph claim.** Figure5 was not visually inspected. Methods gives paste25±1°Brix, Results5.20°Brix; viscosity units differ between measurement and model explanations. Further table/prose conflicts below weaken the usefulness of retaining a disclaimer-heavy paragraph just to say “hotter is not always thicker”. Source3 remains in the review archive, not the corrected article. The sound distinction between processing temperature and evaluation temperature survives as practical guidance, without an unverified peak or factory optimum.[3]
5. **Retain the statistical grouping and qualify the subset.** Explicitly identify omission of untreated-juice Control, preserve all12 displayed cells, same-row interpretation, shared letters and mean±SD. Table footnote p<0.0.5 is malformed; Methods p<0.05 is separately identified, not silently substituted into the source. Triplicates do not prove three industrial batches.[2]
6. **Naturalise and sharpen ENZH procurement prose.** Replace metaphorical “buy a processing history” and repeated disclaimers with what to compare, under which conditions, and when to optimise dose. Chinese uses 浓缩番茄酱 to avoid implying seasoned ketchup, 液相 for serum, 甲酯化度 for this DM context, and 稠度/表观黏度 according to meaning. Describe manufacturer recommendations as positioning, not verified findings. Separate comparable tomato-solids screening from later equal-target formulation optimisation. No stock, certification, cost-saving or shelf-life claims added.[1]
