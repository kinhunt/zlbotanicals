Tamarind Seed Gum
Discover GLYLOID, also known as 
Tamarind Seed Gum, a unique ingredient 
that elevates stability and creates a 'melt in the 
mouth' sensory experience in a wide range of 
food products. With a history of over 60 years of 
successful use in Japan, GLYLOID is considered a 
'clean ingredient,' meeting the demands of 
modern consumers for natural and 
high-quality products. At Socius Ingredients, 
we're excited to introduce you to the 
remarkable beneﬁts of integrating 
GLYLOID into your formulations.
Key Beneﬁts of GLYLOID®
Natural Thickening Agent
GLYLOID acts as a natural thickener, enhancing 
the texture and mouthfeel of your food products. 
From sauces to desserts, Tamarind Seed Gum 
ensures a smooth and uniform consistency that 
delights consumers.
Clean Label Solution
With growing consumer demand for clean label 
products, Tamarind Seed Gum is the ideal 
solution. It's natural, non-GMO, and allergen-free, 
meeting the needs of health-conscious 
consumers.
Enhanced Freeze-Thaw Stability
Experience superior freeze-thaw stability in 
frozen food products, reducing ice crystal growth 
and preserving taste and texture.
Cost-Eﬀective Solution
GLYLOID oﬀers a cost-eﬀective alternative to 
traditional thickeners and stabilizers, allowing you 
to achieve premium quality without 
compromising your budget.
Emulsiﬁcation
Glyloid acts as a natural emulsiﬁer and can help 
stabilize sauces and dressings without the need 
for ingredients such as PGA.
Improved Stability
Say goodbye to product separation and syneresis! 
GLYLOID improves the stability of emulsions, 
plant-based yogurts and cream cheese - 
extending shelf life and maintaining product 
integrity over time.
