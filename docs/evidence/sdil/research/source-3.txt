Details
This measure:
reduces the Soft Drinks Industry Levy sugar content threshold from 5g to 4.5g per 100ml
removes the exemption for milk-based and milk-substitute drinks
This technical consultation will run for 8 weeks and will close on 6 September 2026. You should email your feedback to
sdilconsultation@hmrc.gov.uk
.
