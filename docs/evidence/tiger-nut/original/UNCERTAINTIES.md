# Evidence limitations and original-image review

## Selected direction: exact unresolved issues

1. **Table2 vs prose:** level2 glucose is described as giving best counts across matrices. Tiger nut VEGE061 means level1/2/3 =3.95e8/3.15e8/6.30e8. VEGE022 and053 highest means also level3. No table significance letters; cannot say statistically higher. Table2 exact n unknown; general statistics say3–5 replicates in at least2 experiments. One zero ± term kept literally.
2. **Temperature generalisation:** table2 carob and rice rows do not all have higher counts at37°C. Draft does not reproduce blanket advantage. Separate temperature and sugar trials are not a factorial common baseline.
3. **Time assignment:** glucose-level counts called after fermentation; exact row-specific terminal harvest time not tabulated. Figure2A last tiger-nut point about24h; do not attach an invented precise harvest time to each count cell. pH trajectories not digitised.
4. **Sugar:** 7.5/15g/100mL additions, not residual glucose or finished-drink total sugar. Table6 carbohydrate by difference is not sugar consumption. NF sugar-matching unclear across matrices; nutrition table not used for fermentation-causes-nutrition claims.
5. **Sensory:** methods0–3 intensity; Figure5 radial0–100, conversion unexplained. Untrained11-person preliminary panel is not consumer acceptance. Prose lactic/buttery and cheese/sweat/undergrowth not radar-axis terms. Raw data not retrieved. Rancid explanation is speculative; original tiger nut lines low and close. No decisive strain aroma ranking.
6. **Digestion:** Figure6 original y-axis CFU/mL, ticks3×10³ through3×10⁸; letters a/b within matrix per caption. INFOGEST adds fluid and centrifuges to supernatant bioaccessible fraction. Count decrease not complete-cell mass-balance survival percentage. No human efficacy, colonisation or validated probiotic claim. Only VEGE061 studied at this step.
7. **Antioxidants:** Figure3 tiger nut is LEFT COLUMN (not top row). TSP/ORAC/TEAC distinct, axes differ. ORAC transient24h high; TEAC below baseline; TSP mostly lower except early061. Image labels Trolox µM/L whereas methods µM Trolox equivalents/L; no numeric Trolox conversion reproduced. Not human benefit.
8. **Material:**20% tuber feed undergoes screening, no retained-solids yield. Initial°Brix not glucose assay. Commercial defatted flour not tested in this paper.
9. **Storage/safety:** no storage stability assessment; extra tiger-nut heat treatment reflects starting bioload. No commercial sterility or thermal-process validation. Intended live-culture versus post-fermentation heat-treated route must be decided externally.
10. **Supplier:** catalogue data not COA; no supplier relation or current ZL availability claimed. Page8 defatted nutrition9.1gfat/25gfibre per100g and generic trace-allergen warning visually verified. Extra-fine page5 PDF text extracted but vision failed twice with connection error; no numeric page5 composition/particle size used in draft. Supplier website sterilised version is in development, not all grades sterilised. Document publication date not inferred from file name/copyright.
11. **Evidence breadth:** direct draft uses one fermentation study plus one supplier's original documents. Independent experimental replication not claimed. Carob kefir study PMC7912806 is supplementary discovery only; its whey-containing beverage is not automatically plant-only. Satir paper not retrieved.
