## 1. Fermented tiger nut beverage base — selected

**Reader task:** decide whether to source an extracted base, whole flour or defatted flour before selecting starter/glucose conditions. Distinct from seed-protein concentration, tea stability and generic fermentation-health content.

**Primary evidence:** 2025 original full JATS, six parsed tables, original Figures 2/3/5/6 inspected; supplier extra-fine page and actual 16-page catalogue. Four starter rows show why intermediate glucose cannot universally maximise counts. Acidification, chemical assays, aroma and digestion outcomes remain separate.[1][5][6]

**Original reader value:** a substantive material-to-process explanation, exact counterexample table, supplier form distinctions, and a useful sample enquiry—not an empty checklist. Preserves adverse/ambiguous observations: extra heat treatment, TEAC decrease, digestion losses, unspecified storage stability, sensory scale mismatch, and table/prose disagreement.

**Dedup:** no tiger nut/tigernut/cardamom source hits. Carob already has cocoa/baking article; borrowing carob as the main fermentation theme would unnecessarily revisit a newly published material. Tiger nut is the lead; carob/rice appear only as study controls. Current Quillaja beverage module is unrelated to a fermentation substrate. Proposed standalone blog, no existing or promised URL; inbound from beverages/application guides and enquiry rather than creating an unconfirmed sales SKU.

**Limits:** one fully examined direct fermentation study, not a systematic review. Satir 2022 LWT DOI 10.1016/j.lwt.2022.114164 discovered in the references but not obtained/read, so not cited as corroboration. No actual customer demand, search volume, first-party ecommerce observations, GSC, supplier batch results or independent trials.

## 2. Botanical film: chia mucilage plus oregano/savory oils — deferred, not disproved

**Hypothesis:** antimicrobial activity and a usable food-contact film need different acceptance criteria. Could be a substantive move into food coatings instead of another beverage emulsifier article.

**Original evidence read:** PMC8875529 methods and original Tables 1–3. Film solution contains 1.5% w/v mucilage, glycerol35% and Tween20 15% on mucilage mass, oils0.1/1.0/1.5%v/v; casting is not dipping a real food. Table3 at0.1% has no inhibition detected. Original table contains inhibition below the abstract's claimed38.01–77.66% range: P. crustosum with savory1% gives29.38±1.91, while38.01±3.91 is a halo in mm at1.5%, not a percentage. Original control rows are imported from a previous study, not necessarily concurrent controls.[2]

**Why not selected:** direct application evidence is agar-disc testing, with actual-food validation explicitly still needed. More supplier-grade and real-food evidence needed before a procurement article could be more than a laboratory-film translation. Recent tamarind/psyllium/sunflower content also means another seed-derived structural ingredient would add less portfolio breadth. No efficacy or food-contact permission inferred. No new film figures quantitatively cited; future mechanical-strength claims require actual Figure2 review.

## 3. Spice aroma delivery: cardamom format versus nanoemulsion — deferred

**Hypothesis:** distinguish flavour powder/oil/essential oil before choosing encapsulation, and evaluate aroma rather than calling antimicrobial preservation flavour retention.

**Evidence read:** supplier Nature's Flavors collection distinguishes product formats; fetched full HTML. Original PMC11759640 methods uses mixed ajwain/cardamom oils, Tween80(15%), glycerol(6%), total oils1%w/w and water78%. It is a sausage intervention, not a neutral proof that encapsulated cardamom improves flavour in drinks. Its sensory prose contains contradictory control labels and competing statements for best concentration.[4][7]

**Negative retrieval:** cardamom drying PMC9579224 EuropePMC XML returned invalid bytes; original failed response archived. Web extraction was abbreviated and exposed abstract/conclusion discrepancies in major constituents. Not used for quantitative claims. The 2014 microcapsule study was discovered but no full text obtained; no pretend encapsulation-performance comparison.

**Why not selected:** weaker clean evidence for intended aroma-delivery task; nearby live hops aroma/bitter selection and clove powder humidity pages already cover adjacent format/stability decisions. A sausage preservative story would also require independent safety, nitrite and food-matrix review beyond this brief. Supplier distinctions alone would risk a thin form-comparison page.
