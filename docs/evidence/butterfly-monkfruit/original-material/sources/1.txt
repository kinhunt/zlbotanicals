# Monk Fruit Juice Concentrate
## PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
Our monk fruit juice concentrate is approximately 15-20 times sweeter than sugar, and is a unique all-natural, low-calorie solution for sugar reduction.
Because our monk fruit juice concentrate is regulated as a food, as opposed to a food additive or sweetener, it has a unique positioning as a consumer-friendly, clean-label, fruit juice ingredient which can significantly reduce or replace added sugar.
Like all Monk Fruit Corp’s monk fruit products our monk fruit juice concentrate has a clean sweet taste without bitterness. To learn more about monk fruit and monk fruit juice concentrate visit [www.monkfruit.org](http://www.monkfruit.org) .
