MORE ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Lakanto Classic Monkfruit Sweetener is our original sugar replacement, crafted to bring the sweetness you love with none of the sugar. Made with just two ingredients, it measures cup for cup like sugar, making it easy to swap into coffee, baking, cooking, and all your favorite recipes.

Whether you're reducing sugar or simply looking for a better everyday sweetener, our Classic Monkfruit with Erythritol Sweetener makes the switch effortless.

WHY LAKANTO

The best sugar alternative is one you don't have to think about.
Lakanto Classic Monkfruit and Erythritol Sweetener dissolves smoothly in hot and cold drinks, bakes beautifully in your favorite recipes, and delivers clean sweetness without the bitter aftertaste of many artificial sweeteners.

Perfect for
  • Coffee and tea
  • Cookies, cakes and muffins
  • Smoothies
  • Oatmeal and yogurt
  • Homemade sauces
  • Everyday cooking

From weekday breakfasts to holiday baking, it's the simple way to enjoy more of what you love with less sugar.

HOW TO USE

Swap it just like sugar.
No complicated math. No recipe adjustments. Simply replace sugar 1:1 in your favorite recipes.

Use it in:
  • Coffee and tea
  • Baking
  • Cooking
  • Lemonade
  • Smoothies
  • Oatmeal
  • Fresh fruit
  • Homemade desserts

Store in a cool, dry place and reseal after each use.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Just two thoughtfully selected ingredients.

Non-GMO Erythritol

Provides the texture, volume, and baking performance of sugar with zero calories.

Monk Fruit Extract

A naturally sweet fruit that lets us create delicious sweetness without adding sugar.

Ingredients

  1. Non-GMO Erythritol
  2. Monk Fruit Extract
