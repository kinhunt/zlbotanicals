Compared to conventional dietary apple fibers, » Herbacel® AQ® AFB-250 and » Herbacel® AQ® AFB-200 offer high water binding capacity combined with excellent swelling property at cold and warm temperatures. The use of functional apple fiber is adventageous in water retention, texturizing and anti-caking for confectionary.
