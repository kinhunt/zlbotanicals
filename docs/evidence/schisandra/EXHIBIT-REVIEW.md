# Whole-exhibit review — independent consolidation

Scope: every table present in both submitted ENZH candidates, plus every table selected for the consolidated article and plausible alternative chart types. Neither submitted candidate contained an actual quantitative chart. This is semantic/type review of Markdown exhibits, not rendered/mobile approval.

| Exhibit | Verdict | Reason and implementation constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate A: four-row A/B/C/D yes/no sample-design table | Replace | Combine identity and composition in the consolidated four-row table. Two isolated Boolean columns force readers to cross-reference a second table. Keep A→B and C→D explicitly paired in preceding prose; no A→C acid-removal comparison. |
| Candidate A: two-row table mixing before/after extract percentages and process rates | Replace | Different denominators presented side by side invite recovery arithmetic. Show extract concentrations with SD in one table; put author-reported rates in separately attributed prose. Do not derive recovery from concentration ratios or draw connected yield funnels. |
| Candidate B: commercial-offer / what-it-does-not-settle matrix | Adjust | Keep two examples but remove the column of negatives. The positive format distinction drives the reader's decision; scope belongs in adjacent prose. This is a descriptive manufacturer/retailer matrix, not comparable assay results, ranked suppliers, endorsement or ZL specifications. |
| Candidate B: two-route acid-removal versus lignan-loss percentage table | Replace | Use the unified composition table, retaining reported rates in prose. A grouped bar chart would disguise acid-removal and lignan-loss denominator differences; a 100% stacked recovery graphic would falsely imply an independently validated balance. |
| Consolidated T1: two-column commercial format matrix | Keep | Categorical comparison is appropriate. Exact >9%, source attribution and serving-suggestion scope retained. Neither product's actual chemistry tested. Do not add pricing, efficacy scores or assay-equivalence columns. |
| Consolidated T2: holding time, seven-lignan total ±SD, statistical group | Keep | Four observed time points only. Table exposes uncertainty, units and same-letter later groups better than a smooth rising line. Fresh-weight liquid results stay separate from dry plant tissues. If later visualized, discrete points/error bars only, no interpolated monthly values, trend fit, universal optimum or health axis; this would require a new visual gate. |
| Consolidated T3: A–D treatment and two concentration columns ±SD | Keep | Each column measures concentration in the extract, not a mass balance. Caption states organic-acid equivalence and four-analyte scope. Do not sum the two columns into a composition stack: classes and assay definitions do not establish an exhaustive non-overlapping composition. |
| Seed/pulp contrast (prose only) | Keep as prose | Two scoped numbers adequately establish tissue distribution. A supplier-potency chart mixing these mg/g DW results with μg/g FW syrup or undefined schisandrins percentages would be invalid. No generic seed-to-pulp multiplier. |
| USP versus paper analyte definitions (prose only) | Keep as prose | Names and aliases are more readable in two sentences plus the material difference. No invented synonym mapping; no purity chart based on USP's 90–110% of labelled amount. |
| Source paper calibration tables, sugar-type/alcohol comparison, mouse lethality tables | Exclude from article | Parsed and inspected, not selected. Calibration performance is not the article's purpose; sugar-comparison samples differ from time-series samples; acute mouse lethality is not beverage serving guidance. No safety chart, benefit ranking or low-sugar efficacy inference. |
| Optional process illustration | Not required / not produced | A qualitative schematic could distinguish extraction from later deacidification, but is not necessary to integrate this article. No sankey widths, recovery arrows with invented values, equipment ownership imagery or generated numeric infographic. |

## Finite rendering acceptance

Render exactly three native HTML tables in each language. Preserve mean ± SD, units, b/a/a/a groups and source targets. At mobile width, verify that every cell is reachable and the row's time/sample identity remains visible or unambiguous while scrolling; two-column commercial table should wrap naturally. No numeric charts or external illustrations are needed for this version; adding one is a scope change, not a hidden publication prerequisite.
