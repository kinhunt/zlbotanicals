## Scope and findings

Read both full drafts, opportunity, source manifest and all 11 claims. Independently parsed the two archived original JATS documents, all eight tables and their original cells/headers/footnotes; read the primary bodies, abstracts and disclosures. Read the entire captured manufacturer HTML rather than only application excerpts. Archived PMC HTML is a challenge page, not independent article corroboration. Both archived CP Kelco PDF attempts contain 403 HTML, not a PDF. Nothing from those failed captures supports a recipe. No new network retrieval was needed or claimed.

The original drafts were substantially accurate. Corrections improve balance and precision:

1. **Pressure response is not one-dimensional.** At 0.400%, 100→300 bar takes viscosity 161.10±0.66→201.60±1.08 mPa·s while sedimentation 0.00±0.00→0.75±0.12% and creaming 0.00±0.00→2.46±0.22%. But WHC also improves 92.36±0.23→95.35±0.49%. Added this counterbalance: neither blanket higher-pressure nor blanket lower-pressure advice follows. At 0.100%, higher pressure lowers both separation indices. These are means, not new pairwise significance tests.
2. **No selective four-row implication.** Retained the matched 2×2 subset and identified it as only four of 11 design runs. Explicitly added the three 0.250%/200-bar centre runs: sedimentation zero in all, creaming 0.00/2.46/0.69%. Full table audit preserves axial points 0.038/0.462% and 58.5/341.4 bar rather than treating abstract ranges as the entire design.
3. **Storage means more than sediment.** Added the full nine-month viscosity, sedimentation, creaming and WHC series to each language. Sedimentation is nd throughout, not measured zero. Creaming is nd only months 1–4 and nonzero months 5–9; all numeric creaming entries share a. WHC group changes in month 8. Month 3 viscosity b differs from month 1/2 a, but month 4 bc shares a group with month 3 b and month 5 c: do not claim every consecutive rise significant. Retained n=3 and SD, without converting them to independent batches. Stored viscosity is not stored sensory acceptance.
4. **Methods boundaries.** S1 viscosity is explicitly triplicated on the same sample, at 25°C/100 rpm/R-2, two minutes after immersion; rpm is not a shear rate. Separation assays use 21 days at 4±1°C. Dedicated nine-month Methods 2.14 does not specify temperature; separate sensory preparation is only 24 hours refrigerated. Neither can fill the nine-month gap. S1 is sugar/cocoa/phosphate/sorbate dairy matrix, not a clear acidic or plant-protein beverage; 95°C/4 min is not a safety validation.
5. **Ultrasound boundary.** Corrected “below 40°C” to “did not exceed 40°C,” matching S2. S2 1 wt% tap-water suspensions at pH 7 and 150 s/225 kJ/L are not S1 cocoa milk or factory homogenisation. S2 Table 2 AOAC 991.43 dry-matter measurements are distinct from supplier Table 1 and the IF/AIF/SF fractionation in Table 3. Table 3 CF AQ 1.00→0.25 g/100 g is a fractionation sample adjustment, not the rheology comparison concentration. Soluble fibre content does not isolate pectin or explain the effect causally by itself.

## Additional source conflicts and exclusions

- S1 material composition 40+45+5+12=102 without a common explicit basis. No pie chart, delivered-fibre calculation or nutritional mass balance.
- S1 predicted torque 0.834 in Table 2 versus 0.972 mN·m in prose (also the desirability number); unresolved, excluded from draft. Experimental Table 2 viscosity 197.22 is not month-1 Table 5 viscosity 204.95; no artificial day-zero continuity.
- S1 viscosity model is labelled linear in Table 1 while Results 3.1 calls its ANOVA quadratic. No fitted response surface, optimization recreation, p-value reinterpretation or interpolation is justified here.
- S1 Table 3 labels particle size Z-ave (nm), while Methods also describes d4,3; do not silently substitute one definition. EDS Table 4 is not nutritional mineral composition. Microscopy/SEM/docking do not validate a commercial shelf life or prove all real-matrix protein interactions. Sensory panel is small/trained, with prose noting slight citrus notes and control higher cocoa intensity; no universal consumer-preference or flavour-neutrality claim.
- S2 processing-history wording moves from CF Cl more unrefined to all three fruit materials being pectin-extraction residues. Treat this as author interpretation, not a confirmed current-grade manufacturing SOP.
- S2 WBC equation/variable descriptions are ambiguous. Numeric WBC is not used as a dose calculator or reproducible protocol. GalA, AOAC SDF and alcohol-insoluble fractions are different methods, not interchangeable totals.
- S2 prose gives Pa·s figure-linked viscosity values without a numeric original table; this review has not visually verified figure axes. No numeric ultrasound-viscosity plot or converted ratio is accepted. Qualitative Figure 7 discussion alone is cited explicitly.
- Full S4 also markets upcycling, sugar/fat substitution and ice-cream blend replacement. These are not transferred into the draft as independently verified promises or as evidence today's unspecified NUTRAVA grade equals donated Zest. S3 remains unusable.
