Introduction to NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber
Explore the possibilities and benefits of the first Upcycled Certified® citrus fiber ingredient on the market. With the continued growth in consumer demand for clean label products, citrus fiber addresses the need for nature based, clean label-friendly, and recognisable ingredients in a variety of food and beverage products. It is made from sustainably sourced citrus peels that come from the juice industry. NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber offers unique water-binding, texturising, and stabilisation capabilities, and can be used as a substitute for sugar or fat in many recipes. This fiber is a great ingredient for your product development portfolio. (Upcycled Certified® is a registered trademark of
Where Food Comes From

NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber application areas
General information
If you are looking to enhance texture, stabilise, suspend, and improve water binding across bakery, beverages, condiments, fruit applications, meat & seafood, and alternative proteins, we have the solution for you.
Bakery
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber helps enhance texture, retain moisture, reduce sugar, and reduce fat or oil for clean-label friendly baked goods.
Beverage
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber can recover mouthfeel, stabilise formulations, and can contribute to fibre enrichment in beverages.
Condiments & soups
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber provides thickening, stabilisation, emulsification, and water-binding for optimal mouthfeel experience in condiments and soups.
Fruit applications
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber is used to improve gelation, reduce syneresis, and enhance natural fruit textures.
Ice cream & plant-based ice cream alternatives
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber can act as a single ingredient alternative to industry standard blends while providing freeze/thaw stability, melt resistance and a creamy, indulgent mouthfeel (and you can still achieve target overrun).
Meat & seafood
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber aids in binding water, improving juiciness, and enhancing texture in meat and seafood applications.
Alternative protein
NUTRAVA® Citrus Fiber improves texture, moisture retention, and stabilization for alternative protein applications.
