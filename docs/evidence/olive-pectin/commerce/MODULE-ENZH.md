## Three olive-leaf products, three different formulation briefs

NOW, Wild & Organic and Island Nutrition show how much can sit behind the same “olive leaf extract” name. The quantities and ingredient descriptions below come from their Amazon gallery labels, not independent batch testing.

### NOW: a straightforward leaf-extract capsule

[NOW Olive Leaf Extract, 120 vegetarian capsules](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0019LPMDY) pairs **500 mg olive leaf extract per one-capsule serving** with a **minimum 6% oleuropein specification for that extract**. Its other ingredients are hypromellose for the cellulose capsule, vegetable-source stearic acid and silicon dioxide.

The useful feature is the separation of extract quantity from marker concentration. “500 mg” describes how much extract is included; “minimum 6%” describes a constituent specification within it. For a comparable capsule concept, both belong in the material brief, alongside the capsule shell and processing aids—not just the front-label milligram figure.

### Wild & Organic: a liquid built around a glycerin–water carrier

[Wild & Organic Olive Leaf Liquid Drops, 4 fl oz / 120 ml](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C15XRRDJ) declares **500 mg organic olive leaf extract per 0.7 ml serving**, with **certified organic vegetable glycerin and purified water** as its other ingredients. The label describes the product as alcohol-free and sugar-free, while the gallery emphasizes glycerin’s sweetness and mixing into drinks.

Neither an extraction ratio nor an oleuropein standardization appears on the displayed label. Its “500 mg” therefore does not provide the same comparison basis as NOW’s extract amount plus marker specification. Here the development brief also needs to establish what the declared extract quantity represents, how consistently the dropper delivers the intended volume, and how the carrier tastes after dilution. “Liquid” and “drops” describe this one format, not two separate product categories.

### Island Nutrition: leaf and fruit extracts with separate specifications

[Island Nutrition Spanish Olive Leaf Extract, 90 vegetarian capsules](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B010YH0BS4) combines **500 mg olive leaf extract and 50 mg olive fruit extract per one-capsule serving**. The **leaf extract** is standardized to **at least 25% oleuropein and at least 40% total olive polyphenols**. The **fruit extract** has its own specifications: **at least 16% pure hydroxytyrosol and at least 20% total hydroxytyrosol complex**. Other ingredients are a plant-based cellulose capsule and rice fiber.

Each percentage belongs to its corresponding extract, not to the whole capsule or the combined extract weight. Oleuropein and total olive polyphenols are not separate quantities to add together; likewise, “hydroxytyrosol complex” should not be shortened to “pure hydroxytyrosol.” The rear label also separates “100% Grown and Extracted in Spain” from “Encapsulated and Bottled in USA,” making the distinction between raw-material processing and finished-product assembly explicit.

These examples point to two independent choices: **product format** and **extract composition**. A useful comparison preserves plant part, extract amount and serving basis, marker specification, and carrier or other ingredients. That makes the difference between a standardized leaf capsule, a liquid with an unspecified marker level, and a separately specified leaf-plus-fruit blend much clearer than a comparison of headline milligrams alone.

## 三款橄榄叶商品，三种配方思路

同样叫“橄榄叶提取物”，NOW、Wild & Organic 和 Island Nutrition 的配方信息却各有侧重。以下用量与配料信息均来自各商品 Amazon 图库中的标签，不是独立批次检测结果。

### NOW：把叶提取物用量和标准化指标分开看

[NOW 橄榄叶提取物，120粒植物胶囊](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0019LPMDY)的每份为1粒胶囊，含 **500 mg橄榄叶提取物，该提取物的橄榄苦苷标准化含量不低于6%**。其他配料为构成胶囊壳的羟丙甲纤维素、植物来源硬脂酸和二氧化硅。

这款产品的特点，是把提取物用量与标志性成分浓度分别写清：“500 mg”回答放了多少提取物，“不低于6%”回答这部分提取物按什么成分指标控制。开发类似胶囊时，这两项都应进入原料规格，再与胶囊壳及加工辅料一起考虑，而不是只比较瓶身正面的毫克数。

### Wild & Organic：甘油与水载体下的液体配方

[Wild & Organic 橄榄叶滴剂，4液体盎司／120 ml](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C15XRRDJ)标示 **每份0.7 ml，含500 mg有机橄榄叶提取物**；其他配料为**经有机认证的植物甘油和纯化水**。标签标有不含酒精、无糖，图库则突出甘油带来的甜味及加入饮品的使用场景。

所展示的标签没有给出提取比例或橄榄苦苷标准化指标，因此，这里的“500 mg”与 NOW 的“提取物用量＋标志性成分指标”并不构成同样完整的比较依据。液体产品开发还需要问清这个提取物标示量的具体含义、滴管取液量的一致性，以及稀释后的载体口感。“液体”和“滴剂”在这里说的是同一种产品形式，并非两个独立品类。

### Island Nutrition：叶与果实分开约定规格

[Island Nutrition 西班牙橄榄叶提取物，90粒植物胶囊](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B010YH0BS4)的每份为1粒胶囊，包含 **500 mg橄榄叶提取物和50 mg橄榄果实提取物**。其中，**叶提取物**的橄榄苦苷**不低于25%**、总橄榄多酚**不低于40%**；**果实提取物**另有两项指标：纯羟基酪醇**不低于16%**、总羟基酪醇复合物**不低于20%**。其他配料为植物来源纤维素胶囊和米纤维。

这些百分数分别对应各自的提取物，分母不是整粒胶囊，也不是两种提取物的合计重量。橄榄苦苷与总橄榄多酚不能作为独立含量重复相加，“羟基酪醇复合物”也不能简写成“纯羟基酪醇”。瓶背还分别注明“100%在西班牙种植并提取”和“在美国装胶囊并灌装”，把原料加工与成品加工环节区分开来。

这三款商品呈现的是两个独立选择：**产品形式**与**提取物组成**。保留植物部位、提取物用量及每份基准、标志性成分规格、载体或其他配料，才能清楚区分标准化叶提取物胶囊、未标明标志性成分水平的液体产品，以及叶与果实分别规定指标的复配胶囊，而不只是比较几个醒目的毫克数。
