## Request a goji ingredient quotation

Send ZL Botanicals your application, destination market, preferred material form, sample quantity and expected order scale. For drinks and gummies, specify fruit-solids and carrier requirements; for a polysaccharide-oriented extract, specify the target assay, method and reporting basis. We discuss these as separate sourcing briefs rather than treating equal powder weights as equivalent materials.

If organic certification is required, name the destination scheme so the exact material and supply-chain scope can be checked. Grade, composition, available documentation, certification status and supply terms require written confirmation for the proposed material.
