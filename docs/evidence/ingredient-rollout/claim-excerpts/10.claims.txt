Claims What we claim is: 1 . ) A method for producing a decaffeinated tea product, such as decaffeinated tea leaves, comprising (i) adding water having a temperature of less than 70 degrees Celsius to tea leaves to form an aqueous tea leaf mixture, (ii) separating the tea leaves from said aqueous tea leaf mixture to obtain an aqueous extract and extracted tea leaves; (iii) contacting the aqueous extract of (ii) with an active carbon adsorbent (ACA); (iv) adsorbing caffeine in said extract reversibly to the ACA to obtain an aqueous decaffeinated tea extract (DTE), (v) contacting the aqueous DTE with the extracted tea leaves of (ii) to form a further aqueous tea leaf mixture, optionally, repeating (ii) to (iv) 2 to 12 times, and (vi) drying the further aqueous tea leaf mixture to obtain the decaffeinated tea product. 2. ) The method of claim 1 , wherein in (v), (ii) to (iv) is repeated 2 to 12 times, preferably 4 to 10 times. 3. ) The method of claim 1 or claim 2, wherein the time between adding the water or DTE to form the aqueous tea leaf mixture in (i) or the further aqueous tea leaf mixture in (v) and separating the extracted tea leaves in (ii) is more than 2 minutes, but less than 60 minutes, preferably between 2 and 20 minutes. 4. ) The method of any one of the preceding claims further comprising adding a solvent to the ACA and desorbing said caffeine adsorbed thereto, and optionally reusing the ACA in contacting a further aqueous extract of (ii) with said ACA. 5. ) The method of claim 4, wherein the caffeine desorbed is recovered from the solvent and the solvent is optionally recirculated to said ACA. 1 6. ) The method of claim 5, wherein said caffeine is dried by evaporation the solvent. 7. ) The method of claim 4 or any one of the subsequent claims, wherein said solvent is an alcohol such as methanol or ethanol, or an ester such as ethyl acetate. 8. ) The method of claim 4 or any one of the subsequent claims, wherein more than 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%, 95% or 98% of the caffeine adsorbed is resorbed from said ACA and is dissolved in said solvent. 9. ) The method of any one of the preceding claims, wherein the ACA is part of an adsorption column. 10.
