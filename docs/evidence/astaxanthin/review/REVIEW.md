## Findings and fixes

1. **Drying percentage needed its denominator and assay.** Six values match original PMC7913560 section 3.5 exactly. Section 2.11 defines C2/C1 × 100, immediate post-drying measured content divided by theoretical powder content. Section 2.10 uses solvent extraction and UV–visible absorbance at 478 nm, not HPLC. The revised caption states these facts. It does not describe retention as powder recovery, manufacturing yield, purity, encapsulation efficiency or bioavailability. The source's parenthetical C1 unit, “8.5 mg Astax/g emulsion,” is not sufficiently clear to rebuild a feed-to-powder mass balance; that absolute value is deliberately not republished. Values remain explicitly reported within-study outcomes, not independently validated absolute recovery.
2. **Layer number was over-causal in the original narrative.** Original EN: “The second layer made a meaningful difference at the dryer.” Original ZH: “第二层在干燥环节确实带来了改善。” Source methods 2.3 show formulation dilution during successive layer construction. Replaced with a comparison of tested formulations and retained the composition/dilution limitation. Maltodextrin and 15% total solids remain explicit. “No statistically significant difference” is not rewritten as equivalence or universal optimality.
3. **Short pH performance was too easily read as shelf stability.** Original EN: “The two-layer emulsion remained negatively charged without phase separation over the tested pH range of 3–7”. Original ZH: “在所考察的pH 3–7范围内，双层乳液保持负电荷，未出现分层”. Added the source methods' 24-hour ambient endpoint and separated this from the seven-day creaming results. The source narrative explicitly reports aggregation above pH 4.3 for the tertiary system; retained as a study observation, not a universal chitosan threshold.
4. **Beverage color and storage scope verified.** PMC7070863 Tables 1–3 support ΔE 8.05 ± 0.09 for prototype 3 versus red brand A. Recalculation from rounded mean coordinates gives 8.0506024619, consistent with 8.05. The uncertainty is reported, not independently recalculated. Visibility is attributed to authors, not invented consumer testing. Materials name H. pluvialis oleoresin; method uses homogenization then oleoresin addition/further mixing and ultrasound. Revised sequence reflects that order. The intense-light 30 ± 2 °C versus foil/refrigerated 5 ± 2 °C contrast is confounded; the revised paragraph explicitly says refrigerated color also changed. It excludes contradictory retention/loss percentages, isolated packaging efficacy, microbiological safety and an ambient shelf-life promise. “Isotonic” remains the paper's commercial beverage description, not an independent osmolality claim.
5. **Caseinate–pectin source genuinely supports the bounded process comparison.** Independently parsed PMC11381615 Table 1 rows: selected L = 0.25% caseinate/0.25% pectin; selected M = 0.06%/0.19%; both 0.5% oil. M's 1:3 nominal ratio does not mean higher absolute pectin content than L. Revised text preserves the composition confound and does not repeat the discussion's contradictory higher-pectin explanation. Section 3.2 supports the pH 3 flocculation comparison; section 3.3 distinguishes cloudy macroscopic appearance from calcium-associated microscopic flocculation. No blanket 500 mM tolerance claim is introduced.
6. **Species, lab additive and physical storage endpoint retained.** The first two sources identify H. pluvialis; the third identifies a purchased ingredient supplier but no algal species/ester profile. Section 2.2 explicitly contains 0.02% sodium azide. The original claim-evidence entry labelled “Laboratory preservative” contains stock preparation text but omits that sentence: literal presence alone was not adequate support. Supplemental evidence now includes the complete section, with XML locator and source hash. One-day separation is from astaxanthin-loaded emulsions at room temperature/light (2.5.6, 3.6), not a universal failure time for the ingredient.
7. **No clinical or supplier promises.** All three are laboratory formulation studies. No health benefit, human absorption, clinically effective dose, clinical ranking, authorized colorant use, stocked SKU, vegan/allergen eligibility, delivery time or ZL manufacturing capability is asserted. Development recommendations are prospective tests, not completed trials.

## Reader-first EN/ZH edit

All sentences, headings, table labels and source notes were read in each language, not merely checked for numerical parity. The rewrite keeps the same three-study application argument while removing defensive repetition and the closing procurement-checklist emphasis.

- Original title: “Astaxanthin drinks: design the oil-droplet interface before choosing the powder” / “虾青素饮料开发：先设计油滴界面，再决定做成什么粉”. Diagnosis: presupposes powder although the argument concerns liquid and powder routes; Chinese “做成什么粉” is conversational and imprecise. Revised: “Astaxanthin drinks: choosing interfaces for liquid and powder formats” / “虾青素饮料开发：液体与粉末如何选择油滴界面”.
- Original: “That is a useful development decision, not an argument against encapsulation.” / “这不是说包埋没有价值，而是提醒开发团队先判断哪一层真正有用。” Diagnosis: answers an objection readers have not made. Replaced with the concrete next experiment: evaluate protein–carrageenan before a chitosan step, then reconstitute and observe uniformity through the consumption period.
- Original: “Buy a development route, not an isolated retention number” / “采购应围绕开发路线，而不是单独比较一个保留率”. Diagnosis: slogan plus procurement framing displaces the application task. Revised heading: “Match the development route to the finished drink” / “按成品形态安排开发试验”.
- Original: “The most useful next step is a project brief…” / “下一步最有用的文件，是一份…项目需求。” Diagnosis: another abstract supplier-document conclusion. Replaced with actual liquid-matrix screening and powder-serving reconstitution, followed by a short process-cost comparison.
- Chinese terminology corrected from “复溶” to “重新均匀分散/冲调” for an emulsion-based powder, avoiding an implication of molecular dissolution. “台面上的样品” and “下一段开发工作” removed. The new Chinese paragraphs are written as technical Chinese rather than preserving English rhetorical patterns.
- Necessary boundaries are placed beside the relevant endpoint; one closing market/clinical scope note remains. The article does not promise that every recommended trial is directly measured in these sources.

Final outline: interface and format decision → actual orange-red beverage and package/storage experiment → drying retention comparison with a narrow data table → short pH versus storage behavior → premixing and composition experiment → distinct RTD and reconstituted-serving trials → short eligibility note → unchanged source identities.
