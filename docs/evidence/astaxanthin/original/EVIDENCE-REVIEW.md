# Evidence review and boundaries — astaxanthin

## Selected reader task
Decide whether an astaxanthin beverage project should start with a liquid dispersion or a spray-dried interface design, and understand why extra layers and processing sequence can change application performance. This is not an assay checklist, supplement-health article, free-versus-ester ranking, or catalogue entry.

Three original experimental full texts were downloaded and read through relevant methods/results. Stable numeric citation IDs are in ledger.json, exact verified passages in claim-evidence.json, original XML plus extracted text in sources/. XML bodies contain approximately 51k, 32k and 51k characters respectively. No abstract-only source carries the draft.

## [1] 2020 isotonic beverage, DOI 10.3390/molecules25040841
Material: supercritical-CO2 H. pluvialis oleoresin; sucrose monoester/propylene glycol/sugar/water emulsion added to an existing colorless commercial isotonic beverage. This is original applied beverage research, not a supplier guarantee.

Used: emulsion preparation; closest-match ΔE of 8.05 ± 0.09 and authors' visibly different interpretation; illuminated 30 ± 2 °C versus foil-wrapped refrigerated 5 ± 2 °C comparison; eight-day color loss in illuminated treatment.

Important exclusions: abstract describes approximately 65–76% loss but results/conclusion variously describe concentration remaining around 65–76% and approximately 25% initial loss. Do not quote quantitative retention or a three-month stability guarantee from this source. Light and temperature are confounded, so no isolated light-barrier effect is claimed. Reported light intensity unit is unusual; not reused. Authors call measured image areas 'micelles'; no droplet diameter inferred from area. Health, FDA and recommended daily-intake assertions in the introduction were not relied upon. The phrase 'isotonic beverage' is the paper's product description, not our verification of osmolality. Commercial comparator identities were anonymized in the source.

## [2] 2021 spray-drying interfaces, DOI 10.3390/foods10020312
Material: H. pluvialis oleoresin in sunflower oil, lupin protein (LPI), ι-carrageenan (CA), then chitosan (CHI). Primary/secondary/tertiary refer to interface construction, not supplier grades. All drying feeds also contained maltodextrin; the paper states total solids were brought to 15%.

Used: at 120/160 °C inlet air, retention 88.5/87.4% primary; 96.4/95.3% secondary; 96.8/96.0% tertiary, with no significant secondary-versus-tertiary retention difference. Retention measured immediately after drying; denominator theoretical astaxanthin content in powder, not clinical delivery or storage stability. Secondary emulsion stayed negatively charged without phase separation at tested pH 3–7; tertiary charge decreased and aggregation occurred above pH 4.3. Methods/results and numeric figures as stated in narrative were cross-read; original plotted figures were not independently digitized or visually audited.

Limitations: a laboratory comparison, not a validated production formula. Pre-drying creaming was reduced rather than eliminated; the draft does not call either system unconditionally stable. Feed dilution/interface compositions differ across layer construction. No claim that improvement arises only from layer number, or that retention establishes powder redispersibility. No vegan/allergen/regulatory status is inferred for the multicomponent system.

## [3] 2024 caseinate–sugar beet pectin, DOI 10.1016/j.crfs.2024.100821
Material: purchased astaxanthin, not explicitly specified as algal or by ester profile; supporting mechanism/process comparison only. Sodium azide 0.02% in laboratory stocks excludes copying this as an edible recipe.

Used: the sequence options are protein-first emulsion then pectin versus premixed protein/pectin; selected L ratio 1:1 versus M ratio 1:3; final concentrations also differ (Table 1), so not an isolated order-of-addition causal test. Loaded emulsions phase separated after one day.

Important exclusions: abstract says M systems stable up to 500 mM sodium/calcium, but results distinguish macroscopic appearance from microscopic flocculation (calcium above 100 mM in M). Draft does not repeat a blanket 500 mM tolerance claim. Methods list different pectin amounts for the selected L/M routes, whereas one discussion sentence suggests 'higher pectin content' in M; avoid that mechanism as a resolved fact. No inference of better human absorption from in-vitro digestion, no health benefit, no permitted food-use claim.

## Editorial inferences, not measured findings
- Start with the intended liquid/powder route, then select samples.
- Evaluate two layers before paying for greater process complexity when additional retention benefit has not been demonstrated for this application.
- Build packaging/distribution into the RTD brief and connect dry-powder evaluation to reconstitution.
- Compare cost to achieve the final application, not only raw ingredient price.
These are explicit development recommendations derived from the experiments. They are not ZL trials, measured savings, supplier capability or statements of current stock.

## Verification and remaining gates
Citation CLI --strict --evidence passed both drafts: every cited source exists with verified exact passages and a mechanically generated source list. This checks mapping, not scientific truth. Coverage output is heuristic and undercounts EN citations in paragraphs/Chinese sentences; ZH's reported 100% is not meaningful editorial coverage. No citation-density threshold was used. Both languages were drafted to the same claim strength and self-reviewed; independent EN/ZH review remains outstanding.

No website files, tests, schema, media, product claims or publication were changed. Figures were not visually audited; any reviewer seeking to promote further quantitative endpoints must examine them. No research stock solution is offered as a recipe. Destination-market eligibility for astaxanthin as a colorant/ingredient is unverified, so no universal permission is claimed. If published, preserve the final market-review sentence and avoid a title promising colorant replacement authorization.
