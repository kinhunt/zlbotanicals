What are mushrooms good for?
+
Functional mushrooms have been traditionally used for thousands of years. Scientific research has confirmed the health benefits of these powerful mushrooms; primarily immune support through beta glucans.‡

Since our bodies cannot produce beta-glucans naturally, incorporating functional mushrooms into your daily routine can play a valuable role in nurturing your overall health and wellness.‡

What makes these mushrooms truly special is that each species brings its own unique benefits to your well-being: some enhance memory and focus, others strengthen immune function or promote gut health, while certain varieties help with stress management, provide antioxidant protection, restore balance, or support endurance and recovery‡.
What makes functional mushrooms beneficial?
+
Our diverse offering of functional mushrooms provides a variety of benefits that vary by species‡.

Within these remarkable fungi, you'll find an abundance of beneficial compounds including beta glucans and other polysaccharides, powerful polyphenols, and prebiotic non-soluble fiber—all working together to support your immune system's natural function‡.

Om's carefully grown functional mushrooms are also natural sources of important minerals, an array of antioxidant compounds, and valuable amino acids.

What makes these mushrooms truly special are their unique species-specific compounds: the protective triterpenes found in Reishi and Turkey Tail, the energizing cordycepin that Cordyceps uniquely provides, and the supportive neurotrophic factors present in Lion's Mane. Each of these remarkable compounds contributes to your overall health and wellness in its own special way‡.
What is “mycelial biomass”?
+
Mycelial biomass is the asexual, root-like foundation of mushrooms and is the most long-lived and bioactive phase of the mushroom life cycle.

Mycelial biomass refers to the inseparable matrix of mycelium and the remnants of the fermented, enzymatically-converted substrate that the mycelium of the mushroom is growing upon. Om Mushrooms are grown on nutritious, organic whole oats for periods of time sufficient for the mycelium to digest and assimilate the nutrition contained in the oats and for the mushroom to complete its entire life cycle.

In all our mushroom supplements, we incorporate the whole mushroom (mycelium, primordia and/or fruit bodies) rather than isolating specific parts. This holistic approach allows us to harness the complete nutrient profile that nature intended, ensuring you receive the full spectrum of beneficial compounds that these extraordinary fungi have to offer.‡
