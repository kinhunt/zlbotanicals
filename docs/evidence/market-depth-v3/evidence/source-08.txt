## Frequently asked questions

What's in it, and what's the beta-glucan content?

Six organic mushroom extracts standardized to 29% beta-glucans, at 1,000 mg per serving. That's a 12:1 extract, so it's equivalent to 12,000 mg of raw mushroom. The mushrooms are Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi, Chaga, Turkey Tail and Maitake. In the capsules the only other ingredient is the pullulan capsule itself. No rice flour, no maltodextrin, no grain filler.

How much powder do I use, and what does it taste like?

One scoop a day, any time, with or without food. The scoop is in the tub and there are 60 servings in it, so it lasts about two months. Stir it into coffee, tea, a smoothie, or even soup. It's a hot-water extract rather than ground mushroom, so it dissolves properly instead of sitting on top in clumps. The taste is mild and earthy and coffee hides it completely.

How is this different from cheaper mushroom supplements?

Most budget mushroom supplements are mycelium grown on grain, which means a lot of what you're paying for is starch. We extract fruiting bodies and print the beta-glucan percentage on the label, 29% for this blend. Beta-glucans are the compounds the research is mostly about, so that number is the one worth comparing. If a brand doesn't publish theirs, that's worth asking about before you buy.

Who is Ultimate Mushroom Complex actually for?

It's for people who'd rather take one blend than juggle five separate mushroom bottles. You get Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Reishi, Chaga, Turkey Tail and Maitake in one serving. If you only care about a single outcome, say focus or winding down at night, a single-mushroom product is a better fit and usually cheaper. Skip it if you're pregnant, nursing, or allergic to mushrooms, and talk to your doctor first if you take prescription medication.

How long before I notice anything?

Plan on taking it daily for a few weeks, because this isn't caffeine and there's no same-day hit to wait for. Mushroom extracts reward consistency more than dose size. The people who get least out of them are usually the ones who take them for five days, forget for two weeks, then start over. Setting up a subscription mostly solves that.
