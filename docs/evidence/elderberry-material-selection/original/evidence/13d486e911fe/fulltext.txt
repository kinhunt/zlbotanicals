Health & Nutrition
Our product line stands out through the integration 
of our proprietary IPT (Iprona Polyphenol Technology), 
ensuring high-potency extracts by preserving the 
natural polyphenol profile of fruits without solvents. 
This commitment is matched by our dedication to 
European traceability, guaranteeing that each  
ingredient  is of the highest quality and ethically 
sourced within  Europe. The efficacy and health 
benefits of ElderCraft®,  CherryCraft®, CurrantCraft® 
and AroniaCraft® are clinically proven, offering our 
customers scientifically validated solutions for 
health and wellness. 
These elements define our CraftIngredients, offering 
unmatched quality and authenticated effectiveness.
