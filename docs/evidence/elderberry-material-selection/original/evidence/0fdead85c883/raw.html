Natural fruit colours
We add colour to the product

The colour of your food can really shine by means of our natural colour extracts and colouring foods. For this purpose we use colour-intensive fruits, which we process into highly standardised products.

Discover purCol®
The colourful diversity
of nature

We supply standardised natural colourants with a wide range of intensities for fruit preparations, drinks, sweets, jams and ice cream. Our most vivid colour solution is produced from black elderberry and marketed under the Rubini® trademark. All our natural colourants use industry standard unit quantification.

So you have an idea for an amazing new drink.
Then you're in the right place!

iprona is your ideal B2B-partner. Our experts will work closely with you to develop the just-right flavour, colour and mouthfeel to make your vision a market-ready reality. To ignite the innovation process, we need a clear vision of your idea of the final product, preferably detailed in a written brief. Then, through a collaborative, step-by-step discovery process involving discussions, analyses and tastings, we will work our way to the exact drink you have in mind for your customers. In terms of flavours, anything is possible. Fruity, sweet, exotic, tangy, acidic - you name it, we can make it. The sky’s the limit!

The Art of Winemaking
since 1923

Masters of the art of producing fruit and mulled wine for 100 years. Hitzkopf, a proud family brand at the heart of tradition.

Hitzkopf
A brand with tradition

Our traditional recipes are the very core of our winemaking art. Secrets passed on over generations are combined with modern expertise. With precision and care, we use spices and natural flavours to create a taste experience that makes you feel warm even in the cold season. Hitzkopf‘s flavour bouquet is an ode to the joys of winter and the bond between people - a taste that evokes memories and celebrates traditions.

Large storage capacities
all year round & flexiblel

We can store your products dry, cold or frozen.
iprona Lieboch is one of the largest deep-freeze warehouses in Austria. We have over 100,000 m3 of storage capacity and can shock freeze from +20° C to -20° C.
