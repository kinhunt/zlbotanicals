Light salting and roasting precede mechanical partial defatting of the pumpkin seeds. Further processing and milling produce a fine, olive green to brown powder. It delivers a minimum protein content of 57% with a distinctive flavor from the roasting process. Organic Roasted Pumpkin Protein Concentrate 60 offers a high mineral content and robust nutty flavor profile. It suits organic formulations from Soups and Sauces to Beverages and Baked Goods. For formulators developing premium organic products, it delivers ingredient provenance and mineral density. Its distinctive sensory character makes it a traceable, single-ingredient alternative to conventional plant proteins.
