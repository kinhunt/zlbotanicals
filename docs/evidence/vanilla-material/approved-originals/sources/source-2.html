Description

Twist, sprinkle, and enjoy the delightful touch of ground vanilla powder with Singing Dog Vanilla’s organic Vanilla Bean Grinder. If you love vanilla, you can add real, ground vanilla to whatever foods make you smile. With beans from our family of vanilla farmers in Indonesia, this vanilla powder is an excellent addition to desserts, coffee, tea, smoothies, oatmeal, yogurt, or even hot cocoa. Just twist and enjoy!

Why Choose Singing Dog Vanilla’s® Vanilla Bean Grinder?

Organic Planifolia Vanilla: We only source the highest quality vanilla from Indonesia to fill our vanilla bean grinder.

Ground Vanilla: Our vanilla bean grinder is just real vanilla. “vanilla powder” can come in many different forms but is often made with vanillin (aka fake vanilla) coated maltodextrin.

Fair Trade Plus+®: Make a positive impact with your purchase through our Fair Trade Plus+® initiative, supporting family farmers and their communities.

Grinder Top Jar: This product is packaged in a 1 oz glass jar with a grinder lid, giving it a fine vanilla sprinkling.

About Our Ground Vanilla

Ground vanilla is made with the same organic vanilla beans we use in our extracts. We cut them into smaller pieces and package them in the same grinder jar as our famous vanilla salt. This allows you to grind as much or as little as you want on things like chia pudding, oatmeal, yogurt, coffee, and more.

Key Ground Vanilla Feature:

Strong Aroma and Sweetness: With each turn of the grinder jar top, you can enjoy a strong aroma and floral-like sweetness.

Real Vanilla: The only ingredient is organic vanilla beans (from Indonesia). There are no added sugars, gums, or starches—just 100% vanilla.

Do Good with Delicious Food:

Singing Dog Vanilla is your go-to for enhancing your culinary creations while making a positive impact. Join us in creating delicious food that does good in the world.

Ingredients

Organic Vanilla Beans (from Indonesia)

How To Use

Twist the grinder top to sprinkle ground vanilla on whatever makes you happy: desserts, coffee, tea, smoothies, yogurt, oatmeal, or hot cocoa.

Flavor Profile

classic vanilla
