Cold Water Soluble (CWS) Powders
Leading Suppliers of Instant Tea & Cold Water Soluble (CWS) Tea Powders
Cold Water Soluble Powder is the water-extracted solid of tea that is soluble in cold water. Finlays offers a wide variety of easy-to-use Cold Water Soluble powders. CWS Powder is also sometimes referred to as “
instant tea”.
