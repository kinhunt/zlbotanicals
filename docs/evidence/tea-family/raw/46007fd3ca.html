Black Tea Extract Supplier in India

Black tea extract is crafted from the finest Black Tea Leaves from Assam (India) through a meticulously controlled extraction and drying process. It encapsulates the authentic flavour, aroma, colour, and natural health benefits of strong Assam Black Tea in a highly concentrated and versatile form, making it an ideal ingredient for diverse applications.

At Starlon Naturals Private Limited, India, we take pride in being a responsible, efficient, and globally trusted manufacturer of premium black tea extracts / Instant Tea. By sourcing exclusively from certified gardens and implementing advanced processing technologies, we ensure every batch delivers purity, consistency, and superior performance. Our commitment to uncompromised quality, food safety, and sustainability has positioned us as a preferred partner for leading brands worldwide.

Premium Black Tea Extract Powder for Functional Applications

Starlon Naturals produces high-grade black tea extract powder from selected black tea leaves using a precise extraction and spray-drying framework. The resulting powder features high concentrations of native tea polyphenols and cell-protecting antioxidants. Because of this specific chemical makeup, it operates as a stable, highly effective raw material for industrial functional beverages, commercial nutraceutical formulations, and fortified food lines.

What Is Black Tea Extract?

Black tea extract is a highly concentrated, completely soluble powder derived from processed black tea leaves that keeps essential bioactive compounds like polyphenols and antioxidants intact. Commercial beverage manufacturers, wellness brands, and food production firms rely heavily on this extract. It provides an immediate way to inject robust, authentic flavor, reliable solubility, and health-supporting characteristics straight into large-scale production runs.

Technical Varieties of Our Black Tea Extracts

We manufacture our black tea extracts in specific physical formats to integrate seamlessly into distinct manufacturing systems.

Hot Water Soluble (HWS) Spray Dried

We process these powders specifically for immediate, clean dissolution in hot liquid bases. They are an excellent fit for commercial instant tea premixes, heated nutraceutical drinks, and functional beverage lines that demand absolute flavor stability under high heat.

Know More

Cold Water Soluble (CWS) Spray Dried

Engineered to disperse instantly and cleanly in cold water without clumping or settling. These spray-dried extracts are perfectly optimized for bottled ready-to-drink (RTD) beverages, retail iced teas, refreshing wellness drinks, and modern functional liquid applications.

Know More

Liquid Tea Concentrates

A highly concentrated, fluid extract option built to retain the native aroma, full-bodied taste, and active plant compounds of the raw leaf. It provides a practical, pourable solution for commercial beverage batching, dietary supplements, food processing lines, and bespoke industrial recipes.

Know More

Where Our Black Tea Extract Is Applied

Our wholesale black tea extract supplies vital sensory and functional characteristics across several core consumer goods channels:

  • Ready-to-Drink Beverages: Immediate solubility and a locked-in, stable flavor profile make these extracts highly dependable for commercial iced teas, functional energy drinks, and wellness-focused bottled beverages.
  • Instant Tea Premixes: A balanced ingredient choice that adapts easily to hot or cold packaging lines. It guarantees preparation convenience for the consumer, batch consistency for the packer, and a genuine tea taste.
  • Bakery & Confectionery: Infuses a deep, recognizable tea note into industrial cakes, cookies, premium chocolates, hard candies, and gourmet desserts to deliver natural flavor depth and high sensory appeal.
  • Dairy & Nutraceuticals: Blends natural antioxidants and active tea fractions directly into dairy lines, meal-replacement protein drinks, dietary supplements, and health-focused nutritional powders.
  • Specialty Foods & Custom Blends: Supports ongoing product development in gourmet retail foods, fortified snacks, custom tea blends, and premium culinary formulations that require a distinct black tea profile.
