What is Hot Water Soluble (HWS) Instant Tea Powder?

Hot Water Soluble Powder is the water-extracted solid of tea that is soluble in hot water. A wide variety of Hot Water Soluble instant tea powders are available which are cost-effective and easy to use. HWS powders are sometimes referred to as “Instant Tea”.
