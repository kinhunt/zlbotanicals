## 1. Grape seed extract / 葡萄籽提取物

### Research findings and positioning

Indena's actual ENOVITA supplier sheet lists ≥95.0% proanthocyanidins by spectrophotometry and 5.0–15.0% catechin plus epicatechin by HPLC. It describes water extraction and an orange-brown powder. These are ENOVITA claims, not ZL batch values or proof that ZL supplies the trademarked ingredient.[1] The important sales lesson is a named material plus a method-linked assay, not copying the largest OPC number.

The primary methods paper distinguishes oligomer/polymer distributions chromatographically and says the colorimetric methods offer only crude size-distribution information. A total polyphenol result, a spectrophotometric proanthocyanidin result and an HPLC oligomer profile cannot be relabeled as one interchangeable “OPC purity.”[8] Never write “95% OPC by HPLC” on the strength of the Indena sheet. Seed-derived polyphenol extract is the offer; seed oil, grape skin color and resveratrol remain different material categories.

## 2. Goji berry ingredients / 枸杞原料

This substantiates a commercial juice-powder category, not a ZL organic certificate or a carrier-free specification.[2] Natural Poland's wholesale page lists goji extract 85–95% dry matter and maltodextrin 5–15% dry matter; these figures describe mixture composition, not 85–95% polysaccharides.[3] Do not repeat its unsupported skin-benefit promises.

#### Licorice extract FAQ

**Is DGL the same as glabridin?** No. DGL is a depleted extract mixture; glabridin is a separately assayed constituent. The level of one cannot be inferred from the name of the other.[10]

**Does DGL mean zero glycyrrhizin?** No. The material needs a residual specification and a suitable analytical quantitation limit. A supplier's DGL limit is not a universal specification for all DGL products.[4][10]

**Are glycyrrhizic acid and glycyrrhizinate salt grades interchangeable?** No. The named acid or salt, reporting basis and intended application belong in the material specification; the supplier catalog lists them as different products.[4]

**Can a glabridin powder go directly into water?** Do not assume it will remain dissolved. A studied glabridin stock precipitated after dilution into an aqueous medium, making final-base evaluation necessary.[11]

**What documents, MOQ and lead time can you provide?** Request technical documents and sample arrangements with your selected material brief. Applicable certification scope, packaging, MOQ and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

#### 常见问题

**DGL 就是光甘草定吗？** 不是。DGL 是经去除处理的混合提取物，光甘草定是需要单独测定的成分，不能根据其中一个名称推定另一个的含量。[10]

**DGL 是否意味着甘草酸为零？** 不是。材料须有残留规格及适用定量限；某供应商的 DGL 限度也不是所有 DGL 产品的统一标准。[4][10]

**甘草酸与甘草酸盐能否直接互换？** 不能。具体酸或盐的身份、计量基准与用途应写入规格，实际供应商目录也将其列为不同产品。[4]

**光甘草定粉可以直接加水吗？** 不能假定始终溶解。研究中的光甘草定母液稀释进入水性介质后出现析晶，因此须评价最终配方。[11]
