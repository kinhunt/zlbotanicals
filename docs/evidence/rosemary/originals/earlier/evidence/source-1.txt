EN-FORT is a powerful, natural antioxidant solution derived from rosemary extract (E392), specifically formulated to meet the growing demand for natural label-friendly preservation. Designed for food manufacturers seeking effective alternatives to synthetic preservatives, EN-FORT helps protect product quality by delaying lipid and protein oxidation, which are key contributors to spoilage, off-flavours, and discolouration. Antioxidant for Colour and Flavour Retention and Stability By stabilising fats and proteins, EN-FORT extends shelf life and maintains the fresh taste, colour, and texture of food products throughout storage and distribution. Its versatility makes it suitable for a wide range of applications, including meat and poultry, marine oils, dressings, cereals, spices, and snack foods. Flexible Formats for Seamless Integration EN-FORT is available in both oil-soluble and water-dispersible formats , making it easy to incorporate into a wide range of food systems, from emulsions and dry blends to liquid formulations. Its consistent  performance helps maintain product integrity, texture and appearance while maintaining a balanced flavour profile. Backed by Kemin’s vertically integrated supply chain and sustainably sourced rosemary , EN-FORT delivers not only technical reliability but also traceability and consumer trust —making it the ideal choice for brands focused on transparency, quality, and label-friendly solutions.
