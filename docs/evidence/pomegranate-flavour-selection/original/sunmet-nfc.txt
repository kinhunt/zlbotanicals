Product Description: Pomegranate Juice is prepared from wholesome raw materials suitable for use in food products. This product 
contains no added sugars, acid, flavor, preservatives, or color. This product complies with the Federal Juice HACCP Regulation 21

recognized by the Orthodox Union.
