## Editorial decision

The original drafts gave too much rhetorical weight to plastic-bottle a* values of 15.3 and 15.2. Merely disclaiming statistical significance did not make that contrast strong evidence. The revision instead leads with the 2014 authors' reported absence of visible refrigerated color change despite substantial monomeric anthocyanin losses. It then uses the 2015 Table 5 result: temperature main effect significant for TMA, not significant for a*, with a significant temperature-by-time interaction explicitly retained.

The article is now an evidence-led explanation rather than a procurement checklist. EN and Chinese were rewritten together; repeated “proposed/not validated” qualifications and internal work-diary phrasing were removed. One compact paragraph preserves the liquid-extract versus powder, preservative, process-safety and shelf-life boundaries. Related-group provenance, source-table defects and health-claim boundaries remain where relevant.

## Table decision

Use a descriptive table of all three color coordinates, retaining the printed ± values. Do **not** label them SD: the inspected primary XML does not define them as standard deviations or standard errors. Do not publish a pigment-retention chart, derived dose recommendation, inferred pairwise P value or commercial-material performance claim. The 2014 visual observation is an author observation, not a consumer panel or proof of unchanged instrumental color.
