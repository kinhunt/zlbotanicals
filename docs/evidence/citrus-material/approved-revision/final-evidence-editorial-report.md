## Material findings and editorial changes

1. **Identity is correct within the stated scope.** Hesperidin is the hesperetin rutinoside, hesperetin is the aglycone, and MGH adds a glucose unit. The primary paper's structural description was cross-checked against EFSA's identity table and the legal MGH definition, rather than relying solely on supplier names.[1][6][7] The pure research monoglucoside is distinguished from a commercial preparation containing residual hesperidin. No new structural, manufacturing or solubility claim about HMC is inferred from its Amazon listing.
2. **Four actual ecommerce samples now lead the substantive body.** Direct ASIN links, identities and mass/serving differences are in the first section, followed by procurement implications. Nootropics Depot's 500 mg/90% basis remains unresolved; NOW's 700 mg bioflavonoids is not a quantified hesperidin dose.[10][11] Nutricost explicitly separates its two-capsule 1,000 mg complex into 500 mg of each ingredient; Supersmart remains seller-described HMC with its 98%/500 mg wording intact.[12][13] No 450 mg, 490 mg or 980 mg result is fabricated.
3. **Glucosyl search evidence stays negative only within the captured window.** All six original search titles were read, not merely the collector's interpretation. None identifies glucosyl hesperidin.[14] The drafts neither verify a glucosyl finished product nor infer marketplace absence, market size, demand or sales. Raw review/rank/price fields are not analyzed.
4. **Assay denominators are separated.** CITRAPEAK MGH 75.0–85.0% and total hesperidin ≥70.0% dry-basis wording was checked directly; the EFSA expressed-as definition prevents adding overlapping percentages.[3][6] EU MGH 75.0–85.0% and hesperidin 10–20% remain material-specific.[7][15]
5. **Aqueous evidence is appropriately bounded.** The partition study is not converted into g/L solubility. EFSA stability conditions—1% w/v, specified pH/temperatures, four-week exception and 88% recovery—were checked against the complete paragraph.[1][6] The protein study's trehalose, co-precipitation process and pH 7/24-hour assay were checked against Methods 3.2, 3.6 and 3.7, not inferred from its abstract.[2] No unlike-denominator chart was produced.
6. **The copy is more direct.** Removed research-diary wording, inaccessible-proprietary-report narration, the cosmetic-solubility brochure detour and repeated ZL non-offer disclaimers. No positive ZL offer replaces them. Food/cosmetic grade distinction remains a short, sourced selection point.[5] Chinese was read as Chinese, with material identity, numerical meaning and scope compared to English—not accepted through quote matching alone.
