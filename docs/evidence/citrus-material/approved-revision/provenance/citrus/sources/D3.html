CITRAPEAK™

CITRAPEAK™ is a specialized compound of glucose and hesperidin, a bioflavonoid naturally found in citrus fruits. By combining hesperidin with glucose, CITRAPEAK™ achieves significantly improved water solubility, overcoming the conventional limitations of citrus flavonoids. This superior solubility expands its applicability across a diverse range of formulations and improves its absorbability into the body, maximizing the physiological benefits of hesperidin.

Contact Us

Chemical Name:

Glucosyl Hesperidin

Applications & Uses:

  ●  Beverages (water / tea / carbonated drinks / hot cocoa / alcohol / acidic beverages)
  ●  Supplements (protein powder / tablets / gummies / softgels)
  ●  Confectionery (hard candy / chocolate / jellies / jelly drinks / chewing gum)


Benefits:

  ●  High Solubility: Dissolves well in water and alcohol, enabling polyphenol fortification throughout extensive application areas.
  ●  Heat & Acid Stability: Resists decomposition during high-heat processing and remains stable in acidic environments, ensuring a long shelf life.
  ●  Light Resistance: Undergoes minimal decomposition even when exposed to intense light.


Approvals & Certifications:

  ●  Kosher
  ●  Halal
  ●  CITRAPEAK™ is allowed to be used in food in Japan, the U.S. and other countries. Please contact us for information on the regulatory status for any countries of interest.


Quality and Food Safety Assurance:

CITRAPEAK™ is manufactured in facilities certified to the international standards for quality and food safety management systems:

  ●  FSSC22000
  ●  ISO9001

 

Manufacturer:

Nagase Viita Co., Ltd.


Markets:

  • Food & Nutrition

Markets (English):

  • Food & Nutrition

Brand Series (English):

  • Nutraceutical Ingredients

Physical Properties:

Appearance: Pale yellow to yellowish-brown powder
Odor: Slight characteristic odor

Chemical Properties:

Monoglucosyl hesperidin contents: 75.0 - 85.0% (on the dry basis)
Total hesperidin contents: Not less than 70.0% (on the dry basis)
pH: 5.0 – 7.0 (1% solution)

Packaging Properties:

Package: ・1kg Aluminum laminated bag
・10kg Aluminum laminated bag in a carton box
Recommended storage conditions: Store at room temperature, avoiding direct sunlight, high temperature and humidity.
