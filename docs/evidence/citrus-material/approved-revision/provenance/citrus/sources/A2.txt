What Is Functional Dyspepsia? FD is a chronic disorder of the digestive tract. Unlike acid reflux or ulcers, FD has no visible structural cause, yet it can significantly impact a person's quality of life. Common Symptoms of Functional Dyspepsia Functional dyspepsia is often misunderstood or misdiagnosed due to its vague and overlapping symptoms. The key indicators include: Persistent upper abdominal discomfort or pain Early satiety (feeling full quickly while eating) Bloating and excessive belching Nausea without vomiting Mild regurgitation or acid reflux A sense of heaviness after meals These symptoms are typically triggered or worsened by food intake, making everyday eating a frustrating experience. Over time, they can affect sleep, energy, mood, and overall well-being. How GutGard® Targets the Causes of Functional Dyspepsia Functional dyspepsia is believed to result from multiple overlapping issues such as gastric motility disorders, visceral hypersensitivity, low-grade inflammation, and impaired gut-brain communication. Here’s how GutGard® helps address these root causes: 1. Acts on Gastric Inflammation One of the most important actions of GutGard® is its ability to support low-grade gastric inflammation. The flavonoids in GutGard® exhibit natural anti-inflammatory properties, calming the digestive tract and reducing irritation that may lead to bloating, pain, or discomfort. If you’re also dealing with reflux or heartburn alongside stomach discomfort, GutGard® may help with that too. Read how it supports GERD and acid reflux . 2. Restores Gut Lining Integrity GutGard® helps soothe the gastric mucosa, which may be compromised in those with FD. This protective action contributes to reduced symptoms like nausea and post-meal discomfort. The herb’s antioxidant nature supports cellular repair in the stomach lining. 3. Regulates Gastric Motility One of the hallmark symptoms of functional dyspepsia is delayed gastric emptying. GutGard® has been shown to support smoother digestive flow, preventing that heavy, full feeling after eating. This helps reduce bloating and early satiety. 4.
