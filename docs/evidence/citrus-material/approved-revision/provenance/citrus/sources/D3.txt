Home Products CITRAPEAK™ CITRAPEAK™ CITRAPEAK™ is a specialized compound of glucose and hesperidin, a bioflavonoid naturally found in citrus fruits. By combining hesperidin with glucose, CITRAPEAK™ achieves significantly improved water solubility, overcoming the conventional limitations of citrus flavonoids. This superior solubility expands its applicability across a diverse range of formulations and improves its absorbability into the body, maximizing the physiological benefits of hesperidin. Contact Us Product Information Chemical Name: Glucosyl Hesperidin Applications & Uses: ● Beverages (water / tea / carbonated drinks / hot cocoa / alcohol / acidic beverages) ● Supplements (protein powder / tablets / gummies / softgels) ● Confectionery (hard candy / chocolate / jellies / jelly drinks / chewing gum) Benefits: ●  High Solubility: Dissolves well in water and alcohol, enabling polyphenol fortification throughout extensive application areas. ●  Heat & Acid Stability: Resists decomposition during high-heat processing and remains stable in acidic environments, ensuring a long shelf life. ●  Light Resistance: Undergoes minimal decomposition even when exposed to intense light. Approvals & Certifications: ● Kosher ● Halal ● CITRAPEAK™ is allowed to be used in food in Japan, the U.S. and other countries. Please contact us for information on the regulatory status for any countries of interest. Quality and Food Safety Assurance: CITRAPEAK™ is manufactured in facilities certified to the international standards for quality and food safety management systems: ● FSSC22000 ● ISO9001 Manufacturer: Nagase Viita Co., Ltd.
