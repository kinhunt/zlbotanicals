SUNBLOOM® PRO

Sunbloom® PRO is a versatile plant-based protein with remarkable emulsifying properties. Ideal for medium to high viscosity applications such as spreads, sauces, dressings, desserts, and nutrition, this ingredient ensures a smooth, consistent texture, and supports clean-label formulation.

SUNBLOOM® BEV

Sunbloom® BEV is a finer plant-based protein with similar properties to Sunbloom® PRO. This solution has been designed to enhance mouthfeel in low-viscosity products like milk alternatives, or for protein and fibre enrichment in ready-to-mix, ready-to-drink, and fruit preparations.

Benefits

Emulsification

Sunbloom proteins promote the formation of stable, fine emulsions, thanks to the protein nativity and structural fibre support.

Foaming

With remarkable overrun capacity and foam stability, Sunbloom proteins produce fine and dense foams in drinks and promote the incorporation of air.

Water & Oil Binding

Unique water and oil binding properties make Sunbloom proteins suitable for formulations across a wide texture spectrum—from creamy to firm and structured products.

Nutrition

Sunbloom proteins deliver a complete amino acid profile, including high levels of methionine and cysteine, making it an excellent choice for nutritional snacks and protein shakes.
