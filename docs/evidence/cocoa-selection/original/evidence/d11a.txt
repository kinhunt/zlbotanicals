Fruity and smooth, the perfect partner for milk chocolate creations.
Offering a silky, smooth texture and mild chocolate flavor that's bursting with vivid fruity notes, this reliable powder's bright brown color works perfectly in milk chocolate applications.
Flavor
Sweet and roasted cocoa flavors, also with mild citrus fruits. Bright, smooth and mild.
Color
With a bright light brown color when dry, this transforms to a rich, deep brown when wet.
Application & Uses
This cocoa powder offers an optimal combination of bright color, smooth flavor, and milk chocolate sweetness, making it ideal for various applications, including:
Bakery
Spreads
Bakery
D11A is ideal for milk chocolate desserts such as cakes and cookies, boasting a velvety texture and soft, warm chocolate flavor.
Spreads
Given its milder milk chocolate flavor, this cocoa powder has been crafted for exceptional spreads. D11A offers a bright-brown color and well-balanced chocolate flavor.
Certifications & Quality
We pride ourselves on exceptional product quality and sustainable practices.
Kosher & Halal Certification
D11A is Kosher and Halal certified. We value careful preparation aligning with the beliefs of our communities.
Full Traceability
We prioritize full traceability of our cocoa powder products, promoting transparency from farm to facility.
