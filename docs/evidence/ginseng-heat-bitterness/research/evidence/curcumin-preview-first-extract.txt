Development of Transparent Curcumin Loaded Microemulsions by Phase Inversion Temperature (PIT) Method: Effect of Lipid Type and Physical State on Curcumin Stability
The obtained systems were analyzed for transparency, particle size, lipid crystal polymorphism, and curcumin stability at 20 °C up to 120 days. It was found that the maximum lipid content allowing transparent microemulsions (mean particle diameter of around 25 nm) to be obtained was greatly affected by the lipid characteristics.
