# Turmeric formulation and patent evidence

Retrieved 2026-09-11. Website changes are limited to the turmeric component and its new editorial data. Existing shared source URLs are reused in the original citation namespace, not duplicated in the new source list.

## Evidence map

Indena: WO2007101551A2, priority 2006-03-09, filing 2007-02-21, publication 2007-09-13. Applicant Indena; inventors Andrea Giori and Federico Franceschi. Application claims 1–3 concern complexes and soy phospholipid choices; claim 6 describes alcoholic-solvent preparation, concentration and drying.[1]

Theravalues: US10245238B2, priority 2014-05-15, filing 2015-05-14, grant publication 2019-04-02; prior application publication US20170239194A1 dated 2017-08-24. The US granted method and product-by-process language is narrower than the broad abstract: HPMC/HPC, specified ratio, solution preparation and solvent removal. Six inventors are reproduced from the document. Theravalues is labelled original assignee; the page records a later assignment, so no current ownership claim is made.[2]

OmniActive: WO2012156979A1, priority 2011-05-16, filing 2011-07-22, publication 2012-11-22. Two inventors are reproduced from the publication. Claim 1 includes curcumin, antioxidant, hydrophilic carrier and fat and states depression-treatment utility. Claims 7–9 list ingredient options; claim 10 is the solvent/evaporation/pulverization method. No therapeutic effect is endorsed.[3]

MCT study: phase preparation and measured droplet growth support a comparison framework. Its buffer includes sodium azide; it is not an edible formula. Do not copy its concentrations, heat or homogenization settings into a plant beverage recommendation.[4]

JECFA CTA: material differences, curry/sauce uses, alkaline and light instability and sulfur-dioxide bleaching support quality checkpoints. Historical permissions and use levels are not current market authorization.[5]

## Editorial synthesis boundaries

The four finished-product combinations are proposed development choices, not recipes reproduced from these sources. Oat/coconut bases, ginger/cinnamon and coriander/cumin are labelled flavour choices. Premixing, final-product sensory, fill uniformity, moisture and heat-processing checkpoints are proposed trial controls, not reported successful test outcomes. No blend ratios or doses are supplied; the single A/B range is explicitly a US claim-scope detail.

Three distinct families, not three national versions of one family. WO A2/A1 are application publications, US B2 is a grant. No live legal-status, validity, freedom-to-operate or ZL ownership conclusion is drawn. Primary claims and bibliographic metadata were read directly from Google Patents HTML. Initial web search was unreliable and one guessed A1 URL did not resolve; the verified Indena publication is A2.
