About this item
Micro Ingredients offers high concentrated freeze-dried organic lion’s mane powder, real extract from entire fruiting body and mycelium source, retains active polysaccharides.
Each pouch contains 16 ounces of fresh lion’s mane powder, 1 scoop serving size, provides 1,500mg organic lion’s mane from authentic hericium erinaceus, 302 total servings.
Taking daily may promote healthy energy, brain, and immune support in both men and women.
A potent extract from the entire fruiting body and mycelium helps to harness all of lion’s mane nutritional properties, offering essential enzymes, antioxidants, fibers, and cellular nutrients.
Premium non-GMO lion’s mane nutritional supplements. Made without soy, dairy, gluten, fillers, preservatives, flavors, and tree nuts. Ingredients must pass our 3rd party lab tests to ensure a product that is safe, pure, and potent.

Why do you use the entire fruiting body and mycelium?
Utilizing both the fruiting body and mycelium in a lion's mane supplement ensures that a broad spectrum of beneficial compounds are included, harnessing the whole mushrooms nutritional properties.
What does lion's mane powder taste like?
Lion's mane extract powder carries a subtle and unique flavor often described as being slightly bitter with a hint of earthy richness. This pleasantly mild taste allows it to blend seamlessly into various dishes and beverages.
How do I store this product?
We recommend you store it in a well-closed container away from moisture, direct sunlight, and heat to preserve shelf life and potency.
