Safety Information
Reducing caffeine intake gradually can help avoid temporary symptoms like headaches, or nausea or brain fog. In certain groups and people, Lion's Mane and Collagen & Caffeine can occasionally cause digestive discomfort. Side effects, usually minor, can often be reduced with slow dosage increases. Those with allergies to any ingredients should avoid use. Individuals with any concerns, those on prescription medication, or anyone with underlying health conditions should consult their doctor before use. If any adverse or unwanted reactions occur, discontinue use and consult a doctor. This is not a decaf product and individuals who are sensitive to caffeine or wish to avoid it should not consume or should consult a doctor before trying. Should you experience any symptoms such as brain fog discontinue use and consult a doctor. If you have a dairy intolerance make sure to consult your doctor before adding diary based mixes or creamers to your mushroom coffee.
Ingredients
Everyday Dose Mushroom Blend (Lion’s Mane & Chaga Extracts), Proprietary Coffee Extract (45 mg of caffeine), Collagen Protein from bovine, L-Theanine
Directions
Mix one level tablespoon with water, milk, smoothies, coffee, or whatever you like. Depending on your current caffeine consumption habits and needs, you may want to decrease or increase your dose as your body adjusts. Individuals with caffeine sensitivity should start with smaller doses
