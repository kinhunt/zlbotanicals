Safety Information
Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if safety seal is broken or missing.
Indications
WonderSleep is a daily super mushroom and herbal gummy to support rest and relaxation for restorative, soothing sleep and good mood, including Saffron, Reishi, Corydalis, GABA, Chamomile and Passionflower.
Ingredients
Reishi Mushroom Fruiting Body (Ganoderma lingzhi) Herbal Equivalent of 10:1 Extract, GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric-Acid), Saffron (Crocus sativas) (stigma), Passionflower (Passiflora incarnata), Chamomile Flower (Matricaria chamomillia), Corydalis Yanhusuo (Rhizome) Herbal Equivalent of 10:1 Extract.
Directions
Enjoy two gummies daily or as needed. Store in a cool, dry place. Heat during transit may cause stickiness or white cracking on gummies --this does not affect quality, efficacy or safety. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if safety seal is broken or missing. Please consult your physician before use if pregnant, nursing, currently taking medications, have a medical condition or have advanced sleeping disorders.
