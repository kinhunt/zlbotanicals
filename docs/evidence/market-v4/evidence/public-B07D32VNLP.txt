Real Mushrooms Reishi Mushroom Capsules – Organic Red Reishi Extract for Daily Routine & Wellness – 90 Capsules Real Mushrooms Reishi Mushroom Capsules are crafted to fit seamlessly into your daily wellness practices. Each capsule contains 500 mg of hot water–extracted red reishi mushroom powder (Ganoderma lucidum), made exclusively from the fruiting body—without mycelium, grain, starch, or synthetic additives. This clean and concentrated formula supports individuals looking to incorporate functional mushrooms into their lifestyle with intention and consistency. Sourced from certified organic mushrooms, this extract delivers over 25% beta-glucans per capsule, verified through third-party lab testing. Beta-glucans are naturally occurring compounds found in mushrooms and are often used as a quality benchmark in functional mushroom products. This formula is designed for flexibility—take two capsules daily, or open and mix into your coffee, smoothies, or meals as part of your daily routine. If you're integrating adaptogenic ingredients into your everyday rituals, Real Mushrooms offers a convenient and mindful option. Proudly made in the USA in NSF Certified facilities, these capsules are manufactured in alignment with current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP). NSF certification reflects stringent standards for ingredient quality, production consistency, and label transparency.
