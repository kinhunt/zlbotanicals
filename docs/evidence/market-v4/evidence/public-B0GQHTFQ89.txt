Product Description
FreshCap Organic Mushroom Coffee is an instant mushroom coffee made with organic instant coffee and a concentrated blend of six 100% mushroom extracts: Lion’s Mane, Reishi, Cordyceps, Chaga, Turkey Tail, and Maitake.

 Each serving provides 1,000mg of FreshCap’s mushroom extract blend together with approximately 50mg of caffeine from organic instant coffee. The mushroom blend is produced using a 12:1 extraction ratio and standardized beta-glucan content for a consistent formula in every serving.

 FreshCap combines six functional mushroom extracts in one convenient mushroom coffee powder, including Lion’s Mane mushroom extract, Reishi mushroom extract, Cordyceps mushroom extract, Chaga mushroom extract, Turkey Tail mushroom extract, and Maitake mushroom extract. Our finished product is third-party tested as part of FreshCap’s quality process.

 Designed for easy preparation, this instant mushroom coffee requires no coffee maker or brewing equipment. Simply add one serving to hot or cold water, stir until dissolved, and enjoy at home, at work, or while traveling.

 Each package contains 15 servings of organic instant mushroom coffee with 1,000mg of 100% mushroom extract blend per serving and approximately 50mg of caffeine. FreshCap offers a simple formula for shoppers looking for mushroom coffee, organic mushroom coffee, instant mushroom coffee, Lion’s Mane coffee, functional mushroom coffee, or coffee with mushroom extracts.
