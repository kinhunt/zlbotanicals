## Formula Highlights

### Core Sweetener System: Monk Fruit Glycoside V + Stevia Glycoside Synergy

Traditional zero-sugar products often struggle with the paradox of "taste experience" versus "application flexibility." We deploy a **dual-sweetener architecture** combining high-purity Mogroside V (≥50%) via membrane separation with premium Stevia RebA (≥98%), achieving "clean taste + flexible usage + cost control":

| Technical Parameter | Monk Fruit Only | Stevia Only | **ZL Dual-Component System** |
|--------------------|-----------------|------------|---------------------------|
| Sweetness Purity | 94% | 88% | **98%** |
| Bitter Aftertaste | Slight | Pronounced | **Nearly Imperceptible** |
| Dissolution Speed | Room-Temp Fast | Requires Heating | **Room-Temp Instant** |
| Temperature Stability (60°C) | Good | Fair | **Excellent** |
| Cost Flexibility | Fixed | Fixed | **Ratio-Adjustable** |

**Why Dual-Sweetener Architecture?**

1. **Taste Optimization** — Mogroside V delivers rapid onset but quick fade; RebA provides extended mouthfeel, together mimicking sucrose's sweetness curve
2. **Usage Control** — Brands can modulate the ratio based on price positioning (premium: higher MF ratio; value: higher stevia ratio)
3. **Application Versatility** — Works in cold, ambient, and even warm beverage formats
4. **Cost Hedging** — Commodity price fluctuations differ; blending provides cost resilience

### Processing Guidelines

1. **Dissolution Sequence**
   - Dissolve Mogroside V and stevia RebA in 40-45°C water (optimal dissolution temperature)
   - Add green tea extract powder; thoroughly mix
   - Finally incorporate B vitamins and flavoring for uniform dispersion

2. **pH Management**
   - Target pH: 3.0-3.5 (crisp taste, microbial inhibition)
   - Titrate with sodium citrate to avoid over-acidity

3. **Carbonation Timing**
   - After all ingredients blend uniformly and pass filtration, cool and inject CO₂
   - Pressure: 3.8-4.2 bar (adjust per bottle geometry)

4. **Sterilization & Packaging**
   - Recommended: UHT (135-150°C × 2-5 sec) for flavor preservation
   - Cold-fill immediately post-sterilization, seal to maintain stability

5. **Stability Considerations**
   - Storage: 2-8°C (cold chain) or <25°C (ambient)
   - Shelf life: 12 months (cold chain) / 6 months (ambient)
   - Use light-protective packaging (amber glass or aluminum) due to B-vitamin photosensitivity
