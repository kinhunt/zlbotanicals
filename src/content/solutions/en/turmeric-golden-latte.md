## Formulation Highlights

### Core Differentiator: Water-Soluble Curcumin Technology

Traditional curcumin's biggest challenge is **extremely low bioavailability** (only 1-2%)—most passes through unabsorbed. We use **nano-emulsion water-soluble technology**:

| Parameter | Regular Turmeric | Standard 95% Curcumin | ZL Water-Soluble Curcumin |
|-----------|------------------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Water Solubility | Insoluble | Insoluble | **Fully soluble** |
| Bioavailability | 1-2% | 1-2% | **20-50x enhanced** |
| Brewing Result | Suspended sediment | Suspended sediment | **Clear, bright** |
| Taste | Gritty, earthy | Bitter | **Smooth, mild** |

**Technology**: Nano-emulsion technology encapsulates curcumin in hydrophilic carriers, forming stable water-soluble particles that dramatically improve solubility and absorption.

### Core Ingredient 1: Water-Soluble Curcumin — Anti-inflammatory & Antioxidant

Curcumin is turmeric's primary active compound with powerful anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits:

| Specification | ZL Grade |
|--------------|----------|
| Total Curcuminoids | ≥10% |
| Water Solubility | Fully water-soluble |
| Stability | Hot beverage stable (80°C/30min) |
| Color | Golden yellow |

**Mechanism**: Curcumin exerts anti-inflammatory effects by inhibiting the NF-κB pathway while scavenging free radicals to protect cells. The water-soluble form ensures active compounds are fully absorbed.

### Core Ingredient 2: Licorice Extract — Harmony & Sweetness

Licorice is a key component of traditional golden milk, providing natural sweetness and flavor harmony:

| Specification | ZL Grade |
|--------------|----------|
| Glycyrrhizic Acid | ≥8% |
| Sweetness | 50x sucrose |
| Function | Gastric protection, anti-inflammatory synergy |

**Mechanism**: Glycyrrhizic acid's natural sweetness masks turmeric's earthy notes, while licorice polysaccharides protect the gastric mucosa, creating a gentle pairing with turmeric.

### Core Ingredient 3: Ginger Extract — Warming & Enhancement

Ginger and turmeric are botanical cousins—a golden pairing in traditional formulations:

| Specification | ZL Grade |
|--------------|----------|
| Gingerols | ≥5% |
| Sensation | Warming, mild spice |
| Function | Circulation, absorption enhancement |

**Mechanism**: Gingerols provide a warming sensation and promote circulation while enhancing curcumin absorption. In traditional Ayurveda, ginger and turmeric are commonly used together.

### Synergistic Formulation: The Golden Triangle

```
        Curcumin (Anti-inflammatory core)

    Licorice (Harmony)——Ginger (Enhancement)
```

The three components create perfect balance of function and taste:
- Curcumin delivers core efficacy

**Preparation**: Add one sachet to 200ml hot water or hot milk, stir well, and enjoy.

**Suggested Product Specifications**
- Per box: 15/30 sachets (2-week/monthly supply)
- Per sachet: 15g
- Suggested retail: $18-28/box

### Process Guidelines

1. **Pre-blending**: Thoroughly mix water-soluble curcumin with coconut powder for even distribution
2. **Low-temperature drying**: Use spray drying or freeze drying to protect heat-sensitive actives
3. **Sealed packaging**: Individual foil sachets, moisture and oxygen barrier
4. **Quality control**: Finished product curcumin content ≥3mg/sachet, 6-month stability
