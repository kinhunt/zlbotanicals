## Product Overview

Stevia (*Stevia rebaudiana*) is a perennial herb from the Asteraceae family, native to South America. Stevia glycosides—extracted from stevia leaves—are natural high-potency sweeteners, with key active compounds including rebaudioside A (RebA) and stevioside. As a zero-calorie, zero-glycemic-index plant-based sweetener, stevia glycosides are rapidly expanding in the global zero-sugar food and beverage market.
