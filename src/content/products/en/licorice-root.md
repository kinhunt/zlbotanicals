## Overview

Licorice (*Glycyrrhiza glabra*), known as the "King of Herbs" in traditional Chinese medicine, is one of the most widely used herbal ingredients. The root contains various active compounds, with glycyrrhizic acid being the most important, approximately 50 times sweeter than sucrose.

## Key Active Compounds

| Compound | Content Range | Characteristics |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Glycyrrhizic Acid | 4-20% | Anti-inflammatory, sweetness source |
| Licorice Flavonoids | 1-5% | Antioxidant activity |
| Liquiritin | 0.5-2% | Soothing effects |

## Applications

- **Food & Beverage**: Natural sweetener, flavor enhancer
- **Nutraceuticals**: Gastric protection, anti-inflammatory formulas
- **Cosmetics**: Brightening, soothing, anti-sensitivity ingredient
- **Pharmaceutical**: Cough relief, herbal formulation harmonizer
