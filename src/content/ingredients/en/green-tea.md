## Overview

Green tea (*Camellia sinensis*) is one of the most consumed beverages worldwide. Green tea extract concentrates the beneficial compounds from tea leaves, with **tea polyphenols** and **epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG)** being the most important active components.

## Key Active Compounds

| Compound | Content Range | Characteristics |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Tea Polyphenols | 40-98% | Powerful antioxidant |
| EGCG | 15-50% | Core active component |
| Total Catechins | 30-80% | Comprehensive antioxidant |
| Caffeine | 0.5-8% | Stimulant (removable) |

## Applications

- **Nutraceuticals**: Antioxidant, weight management formulas
- **Functional Beverages**: Tea drinks, energy beverages
- **Cosmetics**: Anti-aging, brightening skincare
- **Food Additives**: Natural antioxidant
