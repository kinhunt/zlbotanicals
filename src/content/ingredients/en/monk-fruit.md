## Overview

Monk fruit (*Siraitia grosvenorii*), also known as Luo Han Guo or Buddha fruit, is a perennial vine in the gourd family, primarily cultivated in the Guilin region of Guangxi, China. As a traditional medicinal and edible plant, monk fruit has been consumed for hundreds of years.

The sweetness of monk fruit comes primarily from a class of triterpene glycosides called **Mogrosides**, with Mogroside V being the main sweet component, approximately 150-300 times sweeter than sucrose.
