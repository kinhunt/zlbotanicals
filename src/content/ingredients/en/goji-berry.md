## Overview

Goji berry (*Lycium barbarum*), also known as wolfberry, is a plant in the nightshade family. Ningxia goji berries are renowned as "red gems" and are a precious traditional Chinese tonic. The extract concentrates the essence of goji berries, primarily polysaccharides and carotenoids.

## Key Active Compounds

| Compound | Content Range | Characteristics |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Goji Polysaccharides | 20-50% | Immune modulation, antioxidant |
| Zeaxanthin | 0.1-0.5% | Eye health |
| β-Carotene | 0.5-2% | Antioxidant, vitamin A precursor |
| Betaine | 0.5-1% | Liver protection |
