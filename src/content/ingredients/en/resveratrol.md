## Overview

Resveratrol is a natural polyphenolic compound found primarily in Japanese knotweed (*Polygonum cuspidatum*), grape skins, and blueberries. Due to its potential anti-aging and cardiovascular protective effects, resveratrol has become one of the most sought-after functional ingredients globally.
